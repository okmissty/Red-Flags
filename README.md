# Unhinged

A monster-hunter dating sim. swipe right, stay alive.

you're a monster hunter, undercover on a dating app called Unhinged. 3 of your 5 matches are monsters. the other 2 are just people (very superstitious people). go on the dates, test them with old superstitions like garlic, salt, silver and mirrors, and figure out who's what before the check comes.

guess wrong and you're dessert. stake a human and you lose your hunter license.

Made solo for JamBurgh 2026 with the theme **superstition**.

## how to play

- pick a match and go on a date
- each course you pick one move:
  - **tests** use a superstition to get a reaction and give you a clue, but make your date more suspicious
  - **charm** calms them down but gives you nothing
- check your notes against the hunter's guide and rule monsters out
- hit **make your move** and say what they are (or clear them as human)
- if suspicion hits 100%, it's over

tip: humans react to superstitions too. they're scared of bad luck, monsters are hurt by it.

## controls

- click / tap for everything
- 1, 2, 3 to pick a move
- space or enter to skip text
- esc to close menus
