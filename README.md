# Red Flags

*A monster-hunter dating sim. Swipe right. Stay alive.*

You're an undercover monster hunter on **Unhinged**, the dating app *designed to be deleted. Permanently.* Intel says three of your five matches are creatures of superstition wearing human faces. The other two are just people, and they're pretty odd themselves. Go on the date, test them with the oldest tricks in the book (garlic, salt, silver, mirrors), keep them charmed so they don't get suspicious, and name the monster before the check arrives. Guess wrong, or push too hard, and you may end up as their dessert.

Made solo for JamBurgh 2026 with the theme **superstition**.


---

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser
## How to play

1. Read your handler's briefing, browse your matches on Unhinged, and pick a date.
2. Each course, choose one move:
   - **Tests** use a superstition to get a reaction. Each one adds a clue to your notes and raises your date's **Suspicion**. Tests are tagged Subtle, Bold or Risky.
   - **Charm** moves calm your date down, but give you no clues.
3. Compare your notes with the **Hunter's Guide** and rule out monsters as you go.
4. Press **Make your move** and name the monster, or clear your date as a **human**. You get five moves before the check arrives, and then you have to choose.
5. Guess the wrong monster, or push Suspicion to 100%, and the date ends badly for you. Attack a civilian and you lose your hunter license. You can always try again so no worries if it doesn't end well.

**Watch out:** bios and answers hide red herrings, and the humans react to superstitions too. They're scared of bad luck, monsters are hurt by it. One clue is rarely enough!!

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Click / tap | Everything |
| `1` `2` `3` | Pick a move |
| `Space` / `Enter` | Skip typing, continue dialogue |
| `Esc` | Close a menu |
